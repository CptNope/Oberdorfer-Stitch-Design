## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the distinction of premier independent New England real estate: an intersection of architectural heritage, enduring coastal and historic estates, and contemporary editorial clarity. It is tailored for high-net-worth buyers, sellers, and architectural connoisseurs seeking refined representation across Massachusetts communities—from Boston’s historic neighborhoods and suburban enclaves to Cape and Island retreats.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Editorial Luxury** with **Architectural Minimalism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Serene, grounded, curated, and deeply residential. Surfaces evoke fine linen, lime-washed plaster, and hand-milled millwork rather than cold corporate tech.
- **Visual Tenor:** Generous negative space, disciplined structural alignments, disciplined hair-thin borders, and high typographic contrast between stately literary headlines and precision technical data.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires absolute trust, discerning taste, quiet confidence, and bespoke care.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the natural and built environment of coastal and historic Massachusetts:

- **Primary (`#1E3A2F` - Heritage Forest):** A deep, distinguished spruce and pine tone used for high-prominence brand touchpoints, primary key actions, active filter states, and iconic elements. Tint variants `#2D5244` and `#3B6655` serve for hover transitions and secondary accents.
- **Secondary (`#C2ADA0` - Coastal Sand / Warm Ochre):** A tactile mineral accent for fine divider lines, subtle badge backgrounds, architectural highlight labels, and secondary interactive framing. Tint `#D8C7B5` is reserved for soft hover washes.
- **Tertiary (`#2B3237` - Deep Slate):** Grounded charcoal used for subheadings, captions, secondary button framing, and UI chrome requiring structure without stark contrast.
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Page Canvas: `#FBF9F5` (Warm Alabaster)
  - Card & Container Surfaces: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Bright White)
  - Sunken Sections / Meta Bars: `#F5F2EB` (Milled Cream Linen)
- **Text Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Text: `#111518` (Near-Black Pitch)
  - Secondary Text: `#2B3237` (Muted Slate)
  - Tertiary / Metric Details: `#6A7276` (Weathered Stone)
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#E5E0D8` (Crisp Architectural Mortar), providing hairline structural definitions.

## Typography

The typography system relies on an intentional balance of high-elegance editorial serif headers and ultra-rational, clean sans-serif data points.

- **Headline Font (`Newsreader`):** Selected for its classic editorial cadence, chiseled serifs, and historic literary warmth. Used in optical sizes for property titles, editorial storytelling, partner quotes, and hero narratives. Set in regular or medium weights to maintain lightness; bold serif is intentionally avoided to preserve elegance.
- **Body and Interface Font (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for pristine legibility in property specifications, floor plans, search filtering, and navigation. 
- **Property Attribute Formatting:** Key property metrics (beds, baths, square footage, price) use `Newsreader` for numerals to echo classic architectural brochures, while their units use `label-caps` in uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored in classical architectural proportions, leveraging a structured 12-column grid that emphasizes balance, framing, and deliberate horizontal stillness.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container width of `1360px`, `4rem` (`64px`) page margins, and `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` (`32px`) margins and `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins and `1rem` (`16px`) gutters.
- **Section Cadence:** Sections receive generous vertical breathing room (`5rem` to `7.5rem` on desktop, `3rem` to `4.5rem` on mobile) to maintain an unhurried, gallery-like presentation.
- **Asymmetric Composition:** Editorial segments pair 7-column primary photography with 5-column property details, balancing visual weight against dense text blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle, tactile surface layering and razor-thin architectural boundaries rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Border System:** The foundation of depth relies on crisp `1px solid #E5E0D8` perimeter strokes. Elements sit flush or nestled beside one another, creating an intentional draftsman grid.
- **Tonal Layering:**
  - **Base Canvas:** `#FBF9F5` establishes the foundational warm plane.
  - **Raised Modules (Cards, Modals):** `#FFFFFF` brings focus to featured residences, contact modules, and sticky navigation overlays.
  - **Recessed Areas:** `#F5F2EB` anchors filters, floor plan technical specs, and footer areas.
- **Ambient Lighting (Restrained Shadows):**
  - **Level 1 (Card Rest):** `0 1px 3px rgba(17, 21, 24, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(17, 21, 24, 0.02)`. Barely perceptible; provides natural lift from cream canvases.
  - **Level 2 (Hover & Float):** `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(17, 21, 24, 0.03)`. Tinted softly with heritage forest green to keep shadows organic and warm.
  - **Level 3 (Modals & Sticky Navigation):** `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(17, 21, 24, 0.12)`, paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and an 85% opacity surface.

## Shapes

The shape system employs deliberate, soft architectural radii (`0.25rem` / `4px`), evoking precisely honed limestone or finished millwork. 

- **Containers & Cards:** Strictly `4px` corner radii (`roundedness: 1`), honoring classical symmetry and structured architectural lines.
- **Interactive Pill Accents:** Search filter chips, property status indicators ("Exclusive", "Under Agreement"), and micro-actions feature full pill curvature (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create an ergonomic, human contrast against orthogonal card grids.
- **Image Treatment:** Property imagery maintains crisp `4px` borders or unrounded flush bounds within framed card structures, preventing excessive softening of architectural lines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Forest green background (`#1E3A2F`), text in `#FFFFFF`, with `4px` soft radius or full pill depending on context. Hover state shifts to `#2D5244` with a gentle `0.2s ease` transition. Padding: `12px 24px`. Typography: `label-md`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF` or transparent, border `1px solid #1E3A2F`, text in `#1E3A2F`. Hover fills with a pale tint (`rgba(30, 58, 47, 0.05)`).
- **Text Link Button:** Underlined with a hairline `1px solid #C2ADA0` placed `4px` below text. Transitions to `#1E3A2F` underline and text on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Small caps (`label-caps`), padding `4px 10px`, pill-shaped (`9999px`). 
  - *Active / New:* `#1E3A2F` background, `#FFFFFF` text.
  - *Pending / Architecture Type:* `#F5F2EB` background, `1px solid #E5E0D8`, `#2B3237` text.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped, border `1px solid #E5E0D8`, surface `#FFFFFF`. When selected: `#1E3A2F` background, `#FFFFFF` text, and no border.

### Property Cards
- Composed with a `#FFFFFF` base, single hairline border `1px solid #E5E0D8`, and subtle Level 1 elevation.
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:3 or 16:10 high-resolution photography framed with zero inner padding at the top.
- **Content Area:** Generous padding (`space-lg`), separating property price (displayed in `spec-number`) from address and architectural style details.
- **Meta Grid:** Bed, bath, and square-foot specifications aligned horizontally with light vertical rules (`1px solid #E5E0D8`) separating each attribute.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E0D8`, border-radius `4px`, padding `12px 16px`.
- **Placeholder:** `#6A7276` in `body-md`.
- **Focus State:** Border changes to `#1E3A2F` with no harsh glow, supplemented by a subtle `0 0 0 1px #1E3A2F`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Precision `16px` inputs with `1px solid #C2ADA0` border.
- Checked state fills with `#1E3A2F` bearing an ivory check or dot.

### Property Inset & Neighborhood Guides
- Informational sidebars and advisor quotation blocks set on `#F5F2EB` with a prominent left border `2px solid #1E3A2F` and typography in `Newsreader` italic.
